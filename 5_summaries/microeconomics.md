# microeconomics

- microeconomics studies the behavior of individuals and firms in making decisions regarding the allocations of scarce resources.
- one can only do one thing at a time, which means he's missing out other opportunities. This is called **Opportunity Cost**
- an oligopoly is a market structure, in which a market of industry is dominated by a small number of firms (oligopolists),
oligopholists could form cartels, and therefore reduce competition, increase prices and long term lower overall market output.
