# first-mover advantage

First-Mover Advantage is a competitive advantage gained by initial first moving to acquiring the market.
First-mover advantage enables a company to have strong and memorable brand image.

For example Apple and OpenAI are both first movers, but unlike Apple,
OpenAI's first-mover advantage is weaker, because friction to move to another AI service is almost non-existent.
People easily move to Claude, Gemini, Open Source models or any other AI API provider.
They still have first mover advantage, but it's relatively weak.
Where first mover advantage and memorability of first-moving shows is that many people still call AI's and LLM's just 'Chatgpt'.
